Go-Stop rules

Matgo is Go-Stop for two people, played with 48 Hwatu cards: 12 months with 4 cards each. Go-Stop for three or four is described below.

The cards

  • Brights (광 gwang): 5 cards with a red 光 circle. The December bright is the rain bright (비광).
  • Animals (열끗 yeol): 9 cards. The birds of February, April and August are called Godori.
  • Ribbons (띠 tti): 10 cards. Red ribbons with writing (홍단): months 1, 2, 3. Blue ribbons (청단): months 6, 9, 10. Green ribbons (초단): months 4, 5, 7.
  • Junk (피 pi): 24 cards. Those marked “2×” count double (쌍피).
  • The chrysanthemum animal (month 9) (국진): when you take it, you decide right away whether it counts as an animal or as double junk.
  • Bonus cards (option): three golden cards worth +2, +2 and +3 junk.

How a round goes

  • Each person gets 10 cards, 8 lie face up on the table, 20 form the pile. If 4 cards of one month lie on the table at the start, the cards are reshuffled.
  • On your turn, play one card from your hand. Then the top card of the pile is turned up straight away.
  • If a card of the same month lies on the table, you take both. If two lie there, you pick one (if they are two equally valuable junk cards, the game simply takes one). If three lie there, you take all four. If none, your card stays on the table.
  • The turned-up card is handled the same way.

Bonus cards (option)

  • Tick “Bonus cards” in the waiting or result window. It applies from the next round.
  • If you have a bonus card in your hand, tap it on your turn: it goes to your junk, you draw a card from the pile into your hand and get 1 junk from your opponent. Then you play a card as usual.
  • If one lies on the table at the deal, the person who starts gets it (without junk from the opponent).
  • If it is turned up from the pile, it belongs to whoever turned it up. They get 1 junk from the opponent and turn up another card.
  • If there is a ppeok on that turn, the bonus card stays with the ppeok stack (without junk from the opponent). Whoever takes the stack gets it.
  • Bonus cards are only taken away when the opponent has no other junk. The chrysanthemum animal is never taken.

Playing alone

Tap “Play against the computer” in the waiting window.

Go-Stop for three

  • Choose “Go-Stop (3 players)” in the waiting window. Once three people are there, the game starts. “Fill free seats with the computer” puts the computer in empty seats.
  • Each person gets 7 cards, 6 lie on the table, the rest is the pile.
  • Go or Stop from 3 points.
  • Whenever you get junk from the opponent (jjok, ttadak, clearing the table, bomb, ppeok, bonus), you get one from each other person.
  • Settlement: the winner is paid by each of the other two separately. Pi-bak, gwang-bak and meong-bak are checked for each person separately.
  • Go-bak for three: if only one of the two losers said Go, that person pays alone for both.

Go-Stop for four: selling brights (광팔기)

  • Choose “Go-Stop for four” in the waiting window. Only three people play each round; one sits out.
  • Each person gets 7 cards, 6 lie face down on the table.
  • After the deal, the three people after the starter decide in turn: sell (sit out) or play. The starter always plays.
  • As soon as someone sells, the others play. If the first two say “play”, the third has to sit out.
  • Whoever sits out shows their brights and gets 1 point per bright from each of the three players (2 brights = 6 points). The rain bright alone does not count. This also applies when you have to sit out. (house rule of this app)
  • The 7 cards of the person sitting out are shuffled into the pile. Then the table is turned up and the three play by the rules for three (from 3 points, go-bak for three).
  • A president only counts for the three who play.

Special moves

NameWhat happens
Ppeok (뻑)Your card matches one on the table, but the turned-up card is from the same month too. All three stay on the table.
Snatching a ppeokWhoever takes the three cards with the fourth gets 1 junk from the opponent. Taking back your own ppeok (자뻑) gives 2 junk.
Ttadak (따닥)Two cards of a month lie on the table, you play the third and turn up the fourth: you take all four and 1 junk from the opponent (does not apply on your very last hand card).
Jjok (쪽)Your card stays on the table and the turned-up card takes it right away: 1 junk from the opponent.
Clearing the table (싹쓸이)After your turn nothing is left on the table: 1 junk from the opponent (does not apply on your very last hand card).
Three on the table from the startThree cards of a month were lying on the table (no ppeok) and you take them with the fourth: 1 junk from the opponent.
Shaking (흔들기)You hold 3 cards of a month and no card of that month lies on the table. You show them: if you win, the round counts double.
Bomb (폭탄)3 cards of a month in your hand and the fourth on the table: you play all three and take all four. 1 junk from the opponent, double round. In return you may later twice just turn up from the pile without playing a card (you choose when).

Junk from the opponent: a single (1-pt) junk first; if the opponent has no single junk, a double (2-pt, including Nov/Dec double junk or Sep Cup used as junk) is taken, then a 3-pt bonus card. If the opponent has no junk at all, you get nothing. Shaking and bombs only count for the winner, and only once per month.

Special wins

NameWhat happens
President (대통령)You are dealt all four cards of one month (or you hold three, lay down a bonus card and the card you draw makes four). You choose: win right away with 15 points (with three or four: 15 from each player) or play on and keep the four cards, e.g. for a bomb later.
Ppeok on the first move (첫뻑)A ppeok on your very first turn: you get 1 junk from every other person.
Three ppeok (쓰리뻑)Three ppeok in one round: an instant win with the base points (Matgo 7, three or four players 3).

For these wins only the nagari factor counts, no baks.

Go or Stop

  • If you have 7 or more points after your turn, you choose: Go (keep playing for more points) or Stop (end the round and win).
  • After a Go you are only asked again once your points have gone up.
  • Even if your opponent has said Go, you may choose Go or Stop at 7 points.
  • On the very last turn it is Stop automatically.
  • If you have no card left in your hand, you cannot say Go: it is Stop automatically.
  • If all cards are played and nobody said Stop, it is nagari (나가리): nobody wins and the next round counts double.

Settlement

1 Go: +1 point. 2 Go: +2 points. From 3 Go: (points + 2) × 2, with 4 Go × 4 and so on. Example with 7 points: 8, 9, 18, 36.

Then it is multiplied by each factor that applies:

FactorWhen
Shaking ×2for each month the winner showed
Bomb ×2for each bomb of the winner
Pi-bak ×2 (피박)the winner has junk points, the loser has 5 junk or fewer (their chrysanthemum animal always counts as double junk here)
Gwang-bak ×2 (광박)the winner has bright points, the loser has no bright
Meong-bak ×2 (멍박)the winner has 7 or more animals
Go-bak ×2 (고박)the loser had said Go
Nagari ×2the previous round was nagari (several in a row: ×4, ×8 …)

Points are only counted; no money is involved. The totals are shown at the top of the game.
